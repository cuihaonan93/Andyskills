## Gap test

A defensible gap answers all six questions:

1. What is unknown, disputed, inefficient, unrealistic, or unreproducible?
2. For which systems, populations, conditions, or decisions?
3. Which sources support or contradict this assessment?
4. Why does resolving it matter?
5. What observation would reduce the uncertainty?
6. Could the apparent gap be caused by search coverage, terminology, inaccessible literature, or publication bias?

## Candidate protocol

Write each candidate as `question -> competing hypotheses -> intervention/analysis -> observations -> decision rule`. Include a baseline, leakage/confounding controls, uncertainty, replication strategy, and stop/failure rule.

## Provisional novelty levels

- **Low confidence:** inferred from a few sources or narrow search.
- **Moderate confidence:** multiple databases/queries plus citation chaining, with known limits.
- **Higher confidence:** systematic current search, terminology variants, citation network, related patents/preprints where relevant, and expert review.

No level guarantees originality or publication.
